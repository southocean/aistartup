# AI adoption for small teams: desk research (13 Nov 2026)

## How crowded is it?

Crowded at the top and the bottom, thinner in the middle.

| Tier | Examples | Who they serve | Price level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big consultancies | Accenture, Big Four, Capgemini/Sogeti, Knowit, Netlight, Tietoevry | Enterprise, public sector | Hundreds of thousands of SEK and up |
| Stockholm AI boutiques | Alice Labs, PhaseOne.ai, Lampo AI Automation, Opsio; Nytta AI (Växjö) | Mid-size, some SMEs | ~1,200–2,500 SEK/h |
| Training providers | NobleProg, Active Solution, Hyper Island, Berghs, Microsoft Copilot partners | Anyone buying a course | Per seat / per workshop |
| Freelancers | Upwork, Fiverr, n8n/Make specialists | One-off automations | ~$40–100/h, heavy price pressure |
| Free substitutes | YouTube, internal "AI champion", vendor onboarding | Small firms | Free |

- RevenueBase counts ~192 AI consulting firms headquartered in Sweden (Aug 2026), Stockholm the largest hub.
  That's a data vendor's estimate, not an official count.
- On Upwork, AI & ML is one of the most-bid subcategories (~3,535 proposals in the measured window), while
  AI integration/automation demand grew 90%+. Demand is growing, but so is supply.
- Nearly all pricing data comes from vendors' own sales pages. Verify by asking for quotes.

## Where the gap might be

Hands-on help for 5–50 person, non-tech teams: sit with them, fix their actual week, leave a small tool behind.
Big firms won't take the job and freelancers sell tasks, not change. The catch is that the gap partly exists
because these clients have small budgets and are slow to buy.

## Adoption: how fast and how deep (added 13 Nov 2026)

**Rate**
- Sweden (SCB, 2025): 35% of firms with 10+ staff use some AI, up from 25% in 2024. Small (10–49) 30.8%, medium 49.6%, large 71.9%. Small firms were at 2% in 2019 and 13.6% in 2023.
- 65% of Swedish firms used no AI in 2025, and 57.9% neither used it nor considered it. Top barrier among those who considered it: lack of relevant expertise (74.7%).
- EU (Eurostat, 2025): 20%, up from 13.5% in 2024. Denmark 42%, Finland 37.8%, Sweden 35%.
- US (Census BTOS, 2026): about 18–20% of firms. Rising at firms with 20+ staff, flat under 20. The question wording changed in Nov 2025, so older numbers aren't directly comparable.
- SCB has also published "Artificiell intelligens i Sverige 2026", not yet read.

**Depth**
- Large companies: most use AI in at least one function, but only about a third scale it. Deloitte 2026: 48% added AI without redesigning workflows, and 12% have redesigned at scale.
- MIT NANDA (2025): "95% of gen-AI pilots show no measurable P&L impact". The measure is narrow, but widely quoted.

**Workers vs. employers**
- 89% of workers use AI at work (Study.com). 41% got no tools, training or guidance from their employer (Resume Now). 35% of AI-using SMBs found unapproved AI in their business (Pax8, Q3 2026).
- Most of these are vendor or career-site surveys. Use them as direction, not precise figures.

**Takeaway:** individuals adopted AI on their own, but companies haven't. The bottleneck is expertise and workflow redesign. That is the product, and it doesn't go stale the way tool tips do.
